07:45  Your host

Meet Jeremy Newman.

Jeremy is an attorney in Texas who does extensive work in public policy and likes reading about everything under (and beyond) the sun. He and his wife Addi, a dental hygienist, met through speech and debate in high school. They live in Fort Worth, Texas, and homeschool their four rowdy sons.

11:30  How we wrestle

Our plan is simple.

  1. Ask the hard question

    Clear answers are nice. Clear questions are indispensable.

  2. Hear the other hand

    Give the other side its strongest, most generous case to see what it has to offer.

  3. Scrutinize motives

    Bad motives produce bad questions. Examine motives closely.

  4. Ask again
